Privacy Policy

Local processing

Passwords, PINs, passphrases, hash inputs and Base64 inputs are processed in your browser's memory. They are not stored, logged or sent to our servers, and we do not operate a backend for these tools.

Breach check network request

The breach check hashes your password locally with SHA-1 and sends only the first five characters of the hash to the Pwned Passwords range API (api.pwnedpasswords.com) operated by Have I Been Pwned. Your full password and full hash are not sent. Their service may log standard request data such as your IP address under their own policy.

Cookies and local storage

SecureKit does not set cookies. It uses your browser's localStorage only for your theme choice (securekit:theme), your favorite tool IDs (securekit:favorites) and your recently used tool IDs (securekit:recent). It never stores passwords, tool inputs or generated results.

Analytics and advertising

No analytics or tracking is installed. If Google AdSense is added later, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads, and this policy and the cookie policy should be updated first.

Hosting

Your hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses and requested URLs under its own policy.

Data retention

We do not retain tool inputs because we do not receive them.
