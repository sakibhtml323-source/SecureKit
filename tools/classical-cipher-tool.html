Classical Cipher Tool

Runs locallyFreeNo login

A learning tool for historic ciphers and puzzles. Choose a cipher, set the shift or key, and encrypt or decrypt.

  • These ciphers are trivially broken and offer no real security. Use modern encryption for anything that matters.

FAQ

Is ROT13 encryption?

No. ROT13 only hides text from a casual glance and is undone by applying it again.
