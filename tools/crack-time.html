Password Crack Time Estimator

Runs locallyFreeNo login

Estimates assume 100 guesses/s online, 10 billion/s against a fast offline hash and 10,000/s against a slow hash, averaging half the search space.

  • Real cracking time depends on the hash algorithm, attacker hardware, rate limiting, dictionaries, leaked lists and password reuse. Not a guarantee.

FAQ

Why three different times?

Attack conditions differ enormously. Rate-limited login pages are slow to attack; a stolen database hashed with a fast algorithm is not.
