Enter text or hex bytes to get the common checksum variants at once, matching the standard check values.

  • Checksums catch accidental corruption only. They are not secure against deliberate tampering; use a cryptographic hash or HMAC for that.

FAQ

Is CRC a hash?

It is a checksum for error detection, not a cryptographic hash. Anyone can craft data with a chosen CRC.
