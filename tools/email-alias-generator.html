Email Alias Generator

Runs locallyFreeNo login

Enter your address and tags to get ready-to-use aliases that arrive in the same inbox, or list dot variants for Gmail-style providers.

  • Not every provider or website supports plus addressing, and aliases do not hide your real address. Use a real forwarding service for that.

FAQ

Do plus aliases protect my privacy?

Only partly. They help you filter mail and spot who leaked your address, but the real address is still visible.
