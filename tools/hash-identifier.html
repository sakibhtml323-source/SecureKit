Hash Identifier

Runs locallyFreeNo login

Paste a hash and see which algorithms fit, such as bcrypt, Argon2, SHA-crypt, MD5, SHA-1 or SHA-256, with notes on whether they are safe for passwords.

  • Identification is a heuristic. Many algorithms share a length, so treat the result as a list of candidates, not proof.

FAQ

Why are there several answers?

A 32-character hex string could be MD5, NTLM or MD4. Length alone cannot tell them apart.
