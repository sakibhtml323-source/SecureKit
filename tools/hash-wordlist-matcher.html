Hash Wordlist Matcher

Runs locallyFreeNo login

Paste hashes and a wordlist; each word is hashed locally and compared. It shows which hashes appear in your list.

  • Dictionary lookup only: no brute force, no salts and no slow hashes. Use it for your own data or authorised exercises.

FAQ

Why was my hash not found?

Either the plaintext is not in your list, the hash is salted or slow (such as bcrypt), or it uses an unsupported length.
