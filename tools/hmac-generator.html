HMAC Generator

Runs locallyFreeNo login

Paste the exact message body and the shared secret to compute an HMAC, or paste a received signature to verify it. Prefixes such as sha256= are handled.

  • Keep secrets private. Verify against the raw body exactly as received, because any whitespace change alters the HMAC.

FAQ

What is HMAC used for?

HMAC proves a message came from someone who knows the shared secret and was not changed. Webhook providers use it to sign requests.
