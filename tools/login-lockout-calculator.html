Login Lockout Calculator

Runs locallyFreeNo login

Enter secret strength and your lockout rules to see guesses per day, the time to exhaust a common-password list and the chance of success over your chosen period.

  • This is a simple one-account model. Distributed attacks, password spraying and leaked hashes are not covered and can be far faster.

FAQ

What counts as a good lockout policy?

One that makes online guessing take years for a strong secret and still blocks the common-password list, combined with blocklists and MFA.
