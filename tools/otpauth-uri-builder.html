OTPAuth Link and QR Builder

Runs locallyFreeNo login

Enter the service name, account and a Base32 secret (or generate a random one) to get a link and QR code you can scan with an authenticator app.

  • The link contains the secret in plain form. Do not share the QR code or link, and delete screenshots after enrolling.

FAQ

Which apps accept these links?

Most authenticator apps accept the standard Key URI format, though some ignore non-default algorithms, digits or periods.
