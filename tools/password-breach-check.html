Password Breach Check

Runs locallyFreeNo loginNeeds internet for lookup

Your password is hashed locally with SHA-1. Only the first five characters of that hash are sent to the Pwned Passwords range API, and the match is made in your browser. This tool needs an internet connection.

  • Your password is hashed locally. Only the first five characters of the SHA-1 hash are sent for the breach lookup. Your full password and full hash are never sent to the breach-checking service. This tool does make a partial network request.

FAQ

What does k-anonymity mean here?

The service returns every hash suffix sharing your 5-character prefix, so it cannot tell which one is yours. You compare the suffix locally.
