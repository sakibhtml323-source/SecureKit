TOTP and HOTP Generator

Runs locallyFreeNo login

Enter the Base32 secret from a 2FA setup to see the live code and the seconds remaining, or verify a code with one step of clock tolerance. Matches RFC 4226 and RFC 6238 test vectors.

  • Anyone with the secret can generate your codes, so use this for testing and recovery planning, not as a replacement for your authenticator app.

FAQ

Why does my code not match my app?

The most common causes are a wrong digit count, period or algorithm, or a device clock that is off by more than 30 seconds.
